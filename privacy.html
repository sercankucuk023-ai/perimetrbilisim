Privacy & KVKK Notice

This notice explains how Perimetr Bilişim ("we") processes personal data collected through www.perimetrbilisim.com, in line with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and, where applicable, the EU GDPR.

Data we collect

  • Contact form details: name, company, work email, phone number, company size and your message.
  • Technical data needed to deliver the website, such as IP address and browser type in server logs.

Why we use it

We use your details only to answer your request, prepare a proposal and provide the services you ask for. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract and our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers that help us run this website and email, under confidentiality obligations. Enquiry data is kept for up to two years unless a contract requires longer.

Your rights

Under Article 11 of KVKK you may ask whether your data is processed, request a copy, correction or deletion, and object to processing. Send requests to info@perimetrbilisim.com.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address.

Responsible disclosure

If you find a security issue on this website, please report it to incident@perimetrbilisim.com. We will confirm receipt within two business days and keep you informed while we fix it.
