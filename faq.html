Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers to what clients usually ask first. If your question is not here, just ask.

Ask a QuestionEmergency Line

General

Which companies do you work with?

Mainly small and mid-sized companies and enterprise teams in Türkiye and Europe that need senior security expertise without building a full in-house team.

We are under attack right now. What should we do?

Call our 24/7 line on +90 (532) 000 00 00. Do not switch off affected systems; disconnect them from the network if you can, and keep a note of what you have seen.

Do you only work with Microsoft security products?

No. We have deep experience with Microsoft Sentinel, Defender XDR, Entra ID and Purview, and we also work with Splunk, Wazuh, CrowdStrike, Fortinet and other common platforms.

How quickly can you start?

Assessments usually start within one to two weeks. Incident response starts the same day.

Do you sign an NDA before we share details?

Yes. We are happy to sign your NDA or provide ours before any technical discussion.

How is pricing calculated?

Managed services are priced monthly by scope, projects have a fixed price after a scoping call, and retainers are a block of prepaid response hours.

SOC Support

Do we need to buy a new SIEM?

Usually not. We start with the platform you have, such as Microsoft Sentinel or Splunk, and only recommend changes where coverage is missing.

What happens when you detect a real incident?

The on-call analyst contains the threat where agreed (for example isolating a device or disabling an account), calls your named contact and opens an incident ticket with evidence.

Can you work alongside our internal team?

Yes. Many clients use us for nights, weekends and holidays while their own team covers business hours.

Forensics

Should we switch off affected computers?

No. Disconnect them from the network but keep them powered on if possible. Memory contains evidence that is lost on shutdown. Call us first.

Will the report be usable in court?

We follow chain-of-custody procedures and document every step so evidence can support legal proceedings. Your lawyer decides how it is used.

Can you investigate an employee case quietly?

Yes. Internal investigations are handled confidentially with a small, named team and limited distribution of findings.

Vulnerability Management

How is this different from a yearly pentest?

A pentest is a snapshot. Vulnerability management runs all year, so new weaknesses are found and fixed between tests.

Will scanning disrupt our systems?

Scans are scheduled with your team, throttled for sensitive systems and agreed in writing before they start.

Do you help fix the issues?

Yes. Each finding comes with clear fix steps, and we can work directly with your IT team or vendors.

GRC

How long does ISO 27001 certification take?

For a mid-sized company, typically 4 to 9 months, depending on how mature your current controls are.

What is a virtual CISO?

A senior security leader from our team who works part-time as your CISO: owning the roadmap, reporting to management and coordinating audits.

Do you cover Turkish regulations?

Yes. We work with KVKK and the Presidential Information and Communication Security Guide alongside international standards.

Security Architecture

We already use Microsoft 365 E5. Is that enough?

It includes strong tools, but they are often only partly configured. We review settings such as Conditional Access, Defender and Purview and close the gaps.

Can you review a project before it goes live?

Yes. A pre-launch design review catches security issues when they are still cheap to fix.

Do you implement the design too?

We can guide and review your team's implementation, or carry out selected parts with them.

Still have questions?

Our consultants reply within one business day.
