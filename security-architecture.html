FAQ

Common questions about Security Architecture.

We already use Microsoft 365 E5. Is that enough?

It includes strong tools, but they are often only partly configured. We review settings such as Conditional Access, Defender and Purview and close the gaps.

Can you review a project before it goes live?

Yes. A pre-launch design review catches security issues when they are still cheap to fix.

Do you implement the design too?

We can guide and review your team's implementation, or carry out selected parts with them.
