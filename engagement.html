Ways to work with us

Engagement Models

Choose the model that fits your budget and team. Many clients start with a project and move to a managed service.

Request a ProposalOur Methodology
Options

Three Models

Each model can include any of our five services.

Fixed scope

Project

A defined piece of work with a fixed price and end date.

  • Penetration tests and assessments
  • ISO 27001 or NIS2 readiness
  • Architecture design reviews
  • Forensic investigations

Best for: a clear, one-time need

Monthly

Managed Service

Our team runs part of your security operations every month.

  • 24/7 SOC monitoring and response
  • Continuous vulnerability management
  • Virtual CISO and compliance upkeep
  • Monthly reporting and reviews

Best for: ongoing protection without hiring

Prepaid hours

IR Retainer

Guaranteed response time with pre-agreed terms, ready before you need it.

  • Priority incident response
  • Unused hours convert to assessments
  • Annual tabletop exercise included
  • Pre-agreed legal and contract terms

Best for: being ready for the worst day
