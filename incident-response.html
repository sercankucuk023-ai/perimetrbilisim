First steps

What To Do Now

These actions protect evidence and limit damage while we get started.

Do now

  • Disconnect affected devices from the network (unplug cable, turn off Wi-Fi)
  • Keep affected computers powered on if you can
  • Write down what you saw, when, and who noticed it
  • Change passwords from a clean, unaffected device
  • Call our 24/7 line before contacting the attacker

Avoid

  • Do not switch off or wipe affected systems
  • Do not pay a ransom before getting advice
  • Do not delete suspicious emails or files
  • Do not tell the whole company before containment
  • Do not restore backups before the root cause is known
