Questions

FAQ

Common questions about SOC Support.

Do we need to buy a new SIEM?

Usually not. We start with the platform you have, such as Microsoft Sentinel or Splunk, and only recommend changes where coverage is missing.

What happens when you detect a real incident?

The on-call analyst contains the threat where agreed (for example isolating a device or disabling an account), calls your named contact and opens an incident ticket with evidence.

Can you work alongside our internal team?

Yes. Many clients use us for nights, weekends and holidays while their own team covers business hours.
